Document extraction and legal review

Covers document extraction and legal review; other articles own their distinct mechanisms and evaluation targets.

Download Markdown

On this page

  • Find missing information, not only incorrect statements
  • Establish record integrity before aggregation
  • Diagnose the document pipeline
  • Select and test representations for the task
  • Preserve source errors; measure extraction coverage
  • Test contract invariance and policy controls

A document system can return accurate-looking statements while omitting a critical page, transcribing a table incorrectly or reviewing the wrong person’s file. Evaluate the path from source document to extracted representation to decision: success at one stage does not establish success at the next, and faithful extraction alone does not establish legal correctness.

Find missing information, not only incorrect statements

Check both whether emitted facts are supported and whether consequential facts are absent. In a speaker’s clinical illustration, a plausible headache note omitted jaw pain on chewing, a detail the speaker regarded as changing urgency. This is an illustration of omission severity, not independently validated clinical advice. A claims-review account similarly scores correctness, completeness and conciseness separately; its provider comparisons are snapshots without a complete numerical table or model settings. [1] [2]

A missing source document is a different problem from a missing fact in an available document. Cross-reference notes, scheduled procedures, reports and billing: if a note references a scheduled X-ray but the report is absent, flag the gap for follow-up rather than inventing why it is missing or assuming the procedure occurred. [3]

For legal review, test missing expected clauses explicitly. The requirement must be conditional on the applicable template and supplier type. A team described removing clauses from signed contracts and mixing these mutations with intact contracts that should receive no flag. The agents reportedly missed most removals; the account supplies no denominator, full omission taxonomy or final recovered performance. Intact controls reveal indiscriminate flagging, while deleted cases test detection—not the legal sufficiency of every contract. [4]

Establish record integrity before aggregation

Deduplicating pages and identifying records belonging to the wrong person are separate checks. Similar names can result in an unrelated person’s record being included in a claim, contaminating the analysis and potentially exposing private information. Verify identity and per-claim extraction before combining claims, and establish permission for the intended cross-record use. The case account does not measure how often contamination occurs. [5]

Once those prerequisites hold, aggregated records may help inspect provider networks or costs. Even then, price differences alone do not prove waste: a higher-priced provider might require fewer treatments, and patient outcomes matter. The described network visualization used a redacted subset; proposed savings were not established causal effects. [6]

Diagnose the document pipeline

A structurally valid pipeline can silently miss a scan, segment the page incorrectly, read a block incorrectly or join blocks in the wrong order. One reported architecture separates layout and reading modules around block-level OCR, making segmentation and order distinct things to inspect; no ablation isolates the benefit of any component. Another account describes extraction confidence, bounding boxes and failures being returned to tools to help refine a schema. A bounding box helps a reviewer locate evidence, but a confidence number is not calibrated merely because it is available, nor do diagnostics detect every error. [7] [8]

State where the evaluation begins. A synthetic pipeline generating text and Markdown tests downstream reasoning over already parsed content; it does not test conversion from degraded scans, handwriting or visual checkboxes. Its speaker’s confidence in available parsers is an opinion, not a parser benchmark. Include source-image cases when those ingestion failures matter. [9]

Select and test representations for the task

Plain OCR text may lose the row-column relationships, charts, formatting or reading order on which an answer depends. Compare representations using the same task and source documents: test both whether the representation preserves those features and whether downstream answers use them correctly. A vendor-affiliated account reported better PDF retrieval with a multimodal approach but did not provide the visual leaderboard details or implementation; another reported improved answers with structured parses, without full numerical methods and with one comparison model omitted. Neither establishes a universally superior representation. [10] [11]

Include varied, complex real document types when they reflect the deployment distribution. A speaker recommended character error rate, table structure, equations and language-specific grammar as possible OCR rewards, alongside synthetic documents; exact reward weights and document selection were not supplied. Such component measures need downstream task checks as well. [12] [13]

Preserve source errors; measure extraction coverage

If the objective is faithful transcription, correct a recognition mistake without silently correcting the source itself. For example, a source table may contain an arithmetically wrong total. A model that recalculates it may produce a plausible number but an unfaithful transcription. Preserve the printed value, then surface any suspected arithmetic issue separately for review. A proposed correction layer for OCR errors was not independently measured; translation or transliteration are distinct objectives. [14] [15]

For structured rows, measure precision as correctly emitted rows divided by emitted rows, and recall as correctly recovered expected rows divided by expected rows, using a defined matching rule and reference set. High precision can coexist with low recall when an extractor outputs only a few easy rows and silently drops the rest. Inspect missed rows and their importance, not just the correctness of returned rows. The reported model and harness comparison lacks detailed numbers. [16]

Test contract invariance and policy controls

When a complete gold answer is expensive, metamorphic tests compare outputs under changes that should preserve a particular rule. Reorder independent clauses, rerun the same contract, or change a supplier name or address only where that field genuinely has no bearing on the rule. Unexpected flag changes reveal instability. Passing such tests establishes neither complete coverage nor legal correctness: the same wrong answer can be consistent across variants. [17]

Policy advice needs a separate control test. Define the approved policy, construct cases where an answer could conflict with it, and check the advice a customer would actually receive. Add a policy check or escalation where appropriate, then rerun those cases through the full path to see whether the control intercepts the error. A speaker-retold airline incident involving alleged incorrect post-purchase discount advice motivated such a proposal; the legal account was not independently checked and the proposed controls were counterfactual, not demonstrated prevention. [18]

A related exploratory proposal compares a user’s privacy preferences with contract terms through concrete scenarios, surfacing possible conflicts. This can inform review, but a generated conflict is not a legal determination and does not give the user power to negotiate the terms. [19]
