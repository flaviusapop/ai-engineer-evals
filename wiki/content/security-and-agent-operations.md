A secure agent environment is an operating system of people, tools, data, policies and evidence—not just a sandbox or a prompt. Controls should make actual activity visible, constrain consequential actions, and test whether the environment supports the intended workload. None of these measures alone proves containment or complete security.

## Discover what is running and govern what changes

Build an inventory of actual agent activity, not just approved applications or employee-initiated chats. A gateway collecting session and tool-call records can reveal scheduled, triggered and SaaS-connected agents; map the observed systems to tasks, owners and risks. The inventory covers what its collection points can see, not necessarily personal devices or other uninstrumented paths. One organization reported more than 300 daily agents in a company of roughly 50–60 people, using a broad, unaudited definition; it is an illustration of the possible discovery gap, not a prevalence estimate. [cite:BgLdvF7Qkh8:c01]

Separately, maintain an AI bill of materials covering models, datasets, dependencies and tools. Compare each build with an approved baseline: an unexpected addition should stop the build for review before an authorized baseline update. This governs changes to the deployed inventory, whereas activity discovery asks what is actually being used. The cited presenter showed an inventory interface but did not demonstrate the separate baseline CI action running. [cite:p-2MT8KYZqU:c04] For deeper dependency risks see [[agent-and-dependency-supply-chain-security]].

Restrictions without a workable alternative can push activity into personal accounts or devices. Offer approved tools and a justified exception path, and explain concrete risks such as unauthorized data transfer while retaining enforceable boundaries. The cited workaround stories are anecdotes; the effectiveness of education or any claimed reduction is not established by a controlled study. [cite:ZyC56eV8sVw:c05] [cite:ZyC56eV8sVw:c14] [cite:p-2MT8KYZqU:c05]

## Make runtime activity observable without preserving authority

A run may produce issues, traces or learned artifacts worth retaining after its sandbox is torn down and its credentials and repository access removed. The distinction is continuity of knowledge versus continuity of authority: a later run can use reviewed findings without inheriting old access automatically. The described architecture does not establish a credential-isolation or retention policy, and retained artifacts themselves need review. [cite:ImnZ7s68jw8:c06]

Users may not see every background action. Action logs and a separate monitor can flag unexpected behavior such as an attempt to access secret storage, providing a basis for investigation even when nobody watches continuously. The proposal has no measured alert quality or detection latency and is not a preventive guarantee. [[agent-authority-isolation-and-runtime-controls]] covers enforcement boundaries. [cite:ZyC56eV8sVw:c09]

Visibility also creates privacy exposure. When raw prompts and responses cannot be exported, send categorical evaluation results instead: aggregate signals remain available, but diagnosing an individual failure becomes harder. Private hosting is another option where richer inspection is allowed. Categories and metadata can themselves reveal sensitive information; minimizing raw content is not a privacy guarantee. [cite:A48uhxfxbsM:c15]

## Review artifacts and act on findings

Let developers scan agent skills locally for quick feedback, then repeat current checks at the authoritative distribution boundary. A local pass may be missing or stale by upload time. Backfill the historical catalog too: a new gate cannot retroactively examine artifacts already published. Distinguish numbers of findings from numbers of affected skills, because one skill can contain several findings. The cited first-party program did not establish scanner precision or complete coverage, including across alternative distribution channels. [cite:iKQ78wyJEXU:c04] [cite:iKQ78wyJEXU:c09]

Triage involves two different questions: is the finding valid, and should it block release? In one demonstrated product, an accepted finding is valid but nonblocking, a confirmed finding affects quality gates, and a false positive is marked separately. Those labels are product-specific, not a universal taxonomy. Record the risk decision and remediation owner rather than treating nonblocking as equivalent to invalid; broader release acceptance belongs to [[production-governance-and-release]]. [cite:UW8Qkk7MG8A:c07]

## Protect transactions and changing counterparties

In the described x402 payment-number flow, a buyer retrying after an ambiguous timeout should reuse the same payment number: issuing a new number may create a second independently valid charge. On the seller side, repeated requests with the same verified payment number can otherwise receive multiple valuable responses even though only one payment settles. Shared state should distinguish pending and completed IDs and return the saved result rather than perform independent fulfillment again. These are complementary protections against duplicate charging and duplicate delivery, not interchangeable controls. The seller example does not specify atomic locking; concurrent requests require a concurrency-safe implementation. This retry advice is specific to the described flow, not every payment API. [cite:YU1uD_YkpjU:c02] [cite:YU1uD_YkpjU:c03]

A strict seller allowlist can limit exposure but makes discovery difficult and can become stale as legitimate domains, wallets or prices change. A proposed alternative maintains approved, blocked and probationary categories, with human review of new identities and interactions. The proposal does not define what probation permits or establish a safe review interval; an operator must decide those constraints rather than assuming discovery itself implies trust. See [[payment-and-purchase-security-for-agents]] for the wider purchase boundary. [cite:YU1uD_YkpjU:c09]

## Prepare data, documentation and execution

Before assigning autonomous work, check that the underlying records, people, infrastructure and governance can support the intended outcome. A fluent interface cannot recover missing comparable outcomes from records that never captured them. For large mixed-media records, one described approach preprocesses PDFs and images, extracts information, and human-verifies a summary before higher-level analysis. A benchmark starting with clean data does not validate OCR, extraction or cleanup upstream. [cite:W4pnzOURwHQ:c01] [cite:W4pnzOURwHQ:c08]

Make the working environment usable as well as controlled: clear documentation, understandable data relationships, modular code, reliable tests and an environment that can execute changes. Identify the specific CLI, API and log access needed and resolve production-data governance before granting access. Placing a debugging sandbox inside a private network is one deployment option for sensitive connections, not proof of isolation or compliance. These are readiness considerations, not a validated readiness score. [cite:Se8jHLliLXE:c07] [cite:X6l4lpA0_NY:c09] [cite:9HbzAWnKbo4:c08]

Check documentation through the agent’s actual retrieval path. A page that looks complete in a browser may omit important content for a fetcher if that content appears only after JavaScript runs. Inspect what the agent receives rather than assuming either that all crawlers fail or that a particular page format is universally required. [cite:vy7o1g2iHY8:c10]

## Test the workload through the deployed interface

Test complete workflows through the same interface agents will use, both locally and in the remote deployment environment. A code interpreter and a cloud browser stress different primitives: package execution and PDF creation are not the same workload as opening tabs, filling forms or downloading screenshots. Use generated exploratory workflows to find surprises, but retain deterministic checks and useful oracles. A team’s thousand generated tests were an arbitrary wide net, not evidence of representative coverage or a validated detection rate. Queryable result storage can make large runs manageable; the example’s SQLite usage did not disclose its schema or concurrency safeguards. [cite:k72eLcYpVys:c01] [cite:k72eLcYpVys:c02] [cite:k72eLcYpVys:c03] [cite:k72eLcYpVys:c04] [cite:k72eLcYpVys:c11]

Check artifact integrity, not merely successful API calls. In one test an uploaded image differed after download; the team traced the mismatch to an encoding layer in the tool wrapper. The source does not specify the exact comparison or repair. Browser workloads also ran out of sandbox memory when opening tabs, with no allocation or failure denominator reported. Both failures show why a model-level diagnosis alone can miss environmental causes. [cite:k72eLcYpVys:c05] [cite:k72eLcYpVys:c07]

Exercise filesystem read, write and execute permissions, mounts and persistence; check process creation, installed package discovery and memory under realistic workloads. If a workflow requires a particular tool or skill, preflight it and stop if missing rather than silently changing the task. An instruction-level preflight is not an independently proven security boundary. Failing a workload under a restriction is a reason to diagnose and design an appropriate solution, not to remove the restriction indiscriminately. [cite:k72eLcYpVys:c08] [cite:k72eLcYpVys:c09] [cite:7T-gldvgfpo:c03]

## Separate business guards from policy distribution

Some requirements depend on both arguments and prior state. In a booking demonstration, a pre-execution rule checks dates and capacity and requires recorded payment before confirmation. Test legitimate paid bookings as well as invalid dates and unpaid confirmations: a guard that blocks everything is not correct business behavior. The selected scenarios do not establish that all invariants were covered. [cite:vJukHCIv7Ck:c08]

Changeable rules can be stored outside the agent and delivered by a runtime control service: agent events go to the service and steering decisions return without redeploying the agent. This changes distribution, not the correctness of the policy or the strength of enforcement. The local service was demonstrated; the managed production mapping was a proposal, not a tested complete security design. For independent per-action enforcement see [[agent-authority-isolation-and-runtime-controls]]. [cite:vJukHCIv7Ck:c10] [cite:vJukHCIv7Ck:c11]

When using an external operator assessment, check its scope. A rating of managed GPU clusters concerns that service and its division of operational responsibility relative to peers; it cannot automatically certify the provider’s bare-metal service, where the customer may operate more of the stack. The publisher’s scope description is not itself a disclosed scoring rubric or evidence for a particular rating. [cite:3axY1wz2OvA:c05]
