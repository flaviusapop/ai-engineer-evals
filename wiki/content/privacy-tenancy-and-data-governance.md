Sensitive data governance is a property of the whole data path, not just the model’s answer. Decide who may use each source, which tenant and document a request may reach, where raw and derived records live, and what reviewers can inspect. A successful query or a redacted screen does not by itself answer any of those questions.

## Enforce tenant boundaries throughout the data path

Treat the model’s query as a request, not an authorization decision. Hiding the scope field from the model-visible schema reduces exposure but does not prevent the model from guessing it. Trusted query validation must reject unauthorized access to hidden fields, while trusted code derives the permitted tenant from the authenticated request and independently applies the restriction outside the model-generated query. The described approach is an architectural recommendation, not a penetration-tested authorization implementation. Check scope at every query, and also at retrieval hits and derived computations, where data can otherwise escape the initial filter. In one financial-research design, each hit is reauthorized against its document, citation and value; separate desks within a firm motivate the boundary. The vendor describes this design without a formal isolation proof. [cite:5O6jLtoeKrg:c08] [cite:ORVh4GivEU0:c07] [cite:ORVh4GivEU0:c08] [cite:dZgA6btnb_s:c08]

Choose the isolation granularity deliberately. A shared workspace avoids duplicating definitions, but requires reliable customer-to-session mapping and an additional access layer where workspace resources are shared. Separate workspaces or organizations make the boundary clearer but require synchronizing definitions; account- or project-level separation is another strong-boundary option, not a mandatory cloud layout. Verify actual workspace sharing and inherited role controls for the deployed version rather than treating a vendor description as a security certification. A speaker-reported incident in which monitoring access exposed another customer’s logs and metrics illustrates why even fast, useful services can fail acceptance; the product, test and remediation were not specified. [cite:QSeaZ6qHyXs:c05] [cite:QSeaZ6qHyXs:c07] [cite:DrTdD-ttjCY:c12] [cite:3axY1wz2OvA:c03]

## Authorize use and design for regulated data

Permission to query a source is not necessarily permission to send it to a model, use it for a new purpose, or move it to another provider or region. Identify the source owner, access grantor and intended-use approver; they may be different people. Map primary processing, evaluation, logs and recovery copies before choosing architecture. A proposed recovery design keeps core traces in their permitted region while replicating identifiers and metadata elsewhere. That is a possible availability tradeoff, not a legal exemption: identifiers and metadata may themselves be restricted. [cite:3ZlQk4-knWM:c02] [cite:Bz1HFxQA1o8:c05]

For protected health information, one practitioner recommends stripping sensitive fields at ingestion before a general-purpose data lake, separating production from non-production without data pipes between them, and restricting raw access by role and region. Removing a field before storage reduces reliance on every downstream dashboard author remembering to redact it. The account supplies no de-identification algorithm, re-identification analysis or privacy audit, and does not prove legal compliance. Validate residual risk and applicable requirements for the actual data and use. [cite:YXEqC05WEI0:c01] [cite:YXEqC05WEI0:c02]

## Protect traces and evaluator explanations

The user-facing answer is only one disclosure surface. Prompts, tool results, stored traces, evaluator inputs and judge explanations may contain or reconstruct sensitive information. A fictional banking demonstration masks a configured card number during instrumentation before persistence, but does not measure masking recall or leakage. Restricting raw trace access while allowing a service account to supply derived analysis to engineers reduces direct exposure, yet the derived analysis can itself leak; a customer-hosted data plane does not necessarily mean a customer-hosted control plane. [cite:obfPzbapMIM:c06] [cite:hfEczxdNyvU:c13]

An evaluator may see a raw trace and repeat its content in reasoning even though the application UI hides it. One proposed design retains the score while withholding sensitive explanations from persistence or display; another runs a second judge on redacted input when reviewers need an explanation. Decide separately what is processed, persisted and shown: UI suppression alone is not backend deletion. The first design relies on sensitivity detection and actual non-persistence on the protected branch; the second relies on input redaction and may lose useful context and add cost. No leakage, accuracy, latency or cost evaluation establishes that either design is safe in all cases. [cite:Bz1HFxQA1o8:c06] [cite:Bz1HFxQA1o8:c07] [cite:Bz1HFxQA1o8:c08]

## Memory, communities and personal representation

More information sharing is not always better. A proposed multi-agent evaluation warns that optimizing information diffusion can reward oversharing, while optimizing recall can encourage reuse of stale facts. Measure whether private information remains contained and whether recalled material is still appropriate alongside public-information reach; this is a conceptual proposal, not a measured privacy guarantee. [cite:x4e5O9zN0TE:c09]

When an agent joins a human community, other participants may not expect their conversation to be recorded or processed by it. Establish what participation and data handling mean for bystanders as well as the agent’s owner, while preserving space for human connection. The source raises this concern without specifying a control or legal analysis. [cite:Lrw0jqBNaw0:c09]

Personal personas add a different responsibility: fluent resemblance is not evidence that a system represents a particular person. Assess the personal record available and keep humans responsible for interpreting its gaps. One speaker’s unsuccessful attempt to help a person with advanced dementia lacked a gathered personal archive and took place when that person was largely beyond language; the resulting generic resemblance motivates caution, not a claim about clinical effectiveness or harm. [cite:IJXjTLPzvAU:c13]
